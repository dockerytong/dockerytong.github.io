Linking Zotero with VS Code

Archived note from the earlier notes site; kept as written, not updated.

zotero_vscode

配置vscode和zotero链接

  1. Zotero中安装BetterBibTeX
  2. VScode中安装Citation Picker for Zotero
  3. 从此链接在工作目录中下载zotero.lua文件
  4. 在vscode pandoc插件设置中加入-s --lua-filter=zotero.lua字段
  5. 在markdown yaml中加入 yaml zotero: client: zotero
  6. 开始写作
