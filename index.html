Mineral interfaces and environmental biogeochemistry

Assistant Professor, Research Center for Eco-Environmental Sciences (RCEES), Chinese Academy of Sciences Beijing, China

I work on reactive mineral interfaces in soil, water, and mining-impacted environments: how they form and transform, and how they shape the fate of carbon, metals, and microbial processes. My current systems are iron minerals and semiconductor minerals, organo–mineral coupling, and tailings-to-soil formation.

My route crosses fields that are usually kept separate — environmental engineering, mineralogy, electrochemistry, and computational chemistry. I combine in-operando electrochemistry with DFT and machine-learned interatomic potentials; I develop synchrotron-spectroscopy methods and hyperspectral data-analysis pipelines for geochemical characterization; and I run the omics analyses for my own questions. That integration, not any single technique, is the capability I bring to a collaboration.

I did my PhD with Chuanping Feng at China University of Geosciences (Beijing) on pyrite oxidation under neutral conditions and its use to drive autotrophic denitrification, then spent two years as a Boya postdoc with Anhuai Lu at Peking University on mineral photocatalysis and iron cycling in the euphotic zone. Since 2025 I have been at RCEES, where the interface question is carried into tailings-to-soil formation and organo-mineral coupling.

Where I work

The group is led by Songlin Wu at RCEES and works on mycorrhiza–mineral–heavy metal interfaces and tailings-to-soil formation. Across the group's projects I manage the geochemical side: synchrotron spectroscopy method development, hyperspectral data analysis, and the electrochemical work, on top of the computational side (atomistic modelling, DFT calibration, machine-learned potentials). Microbiology has dedicated members in the group; the omics analyses attached to my questions are mine. I am the person to contact about synchrotron methods, hyperspectral pipelines, electrochemistry, or atomistic modelling in a mineral-interface context.
