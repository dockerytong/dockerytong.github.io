Methods

My route crosses fields that are usually kept separate — environmental engineering, mineralogy, electrochemistry, and computational chemistry. The integration is the capability; the sections below are where it shows up in practice.

Electrochemistry

The core technique: cyclic voltammetry, impedance including dynamic EIS during reaction, chronoamperometry, and in-operando cells that follow a mineral surface while it reacts — separating interfacial charge transfer from passivating film growth and mass transport under realistic pH and electrolyte conditions.

Synchrotron spectroscopy and speciation

Structure and oxidation-state speciation from synchrotron XAS and STXM, paired with XPS, Raman, and infrared spectroscopy. I develop the measurement and data-reduction workflows behind this work and coordinate the geochemical characterization across the group's projects.

Hyperspectral data analysis

Currently the main development effort: software and automated pipelines for preprocessing, cross-modal registration, and spectral unmixing that turn spatially resolved hyperspectral maps into quantitative mineral and organic-matter distributions.

Computation

First-principles modelling of adsorption, lattice response, constant-potential electrochemical interfaces, and spin-polarized transition-metal/lanthanide oxide surfaces (VASP, ABACUS, Quantum ESPRESSO, CP2K; ORCA and Gaussian for molecular and cluster systems), with conformer and cluster search from xTB and ABCluster and ASE-based workflow automation. Machine-learned interatomic potentials (MACE) are used for configurational sampling and site screening prior to DFT refinement.

On machine-learned potentials: I use them for structural prescreening and relative trends, not for final energetics at spin-polarized or redox-active mineral interfaces; every candidate geometry is checked against DFT before energy differences are interpreted.

Microbial community analysis

Amplicon and metagenomic sequencing. The group has dedicated microbiology members; the omics analyses attached to my own questions stay with me.
